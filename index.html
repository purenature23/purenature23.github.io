About Pure Nature

Pure Nature is a short-form video channel dedicated to real, fact-checked stories from the natural world -- deep-sea creatures, rare and obscure species, dinosaurs and other prehistoric animals, and everything in between. Every video is built around one genuinely surprising, real fact, paired with real photos and footage of the actual animal being discussed. Content is written and produced to be fun and safe for viewers of all ages, including kids.

Real facts, always

Every claim is grounded in documented science -- no invented or exaggerated "facts."

Real footage

Media is sourced from properly licensed photo and video libraries of the actual animal in each story.

Family friendly

Written for a general, all-ages audience -- fun and wondrous, never gory or scary.
